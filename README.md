# Yahtzee – Dice Challenge

A digital implementation of the classic Yahtzee dice game, where you can challenge an AI opponent and test your luck and strategic decision-making skills.

Roll the dice, choose which ones to keep, complete scoring combinations, and compete for the highest score!

## ✨ Features

- **AI Opponent:** Compete against an AI player.
- **Classic Yahtzee Rules:** Gameplay follows the traditional Yahtzee rules and scoring system.
- **Dice Rolling Mechanics:** Roll five dice and choose which dice to keep between rolls.
- **Strategic Gameplay:** Make decisions to achieve the best possible scoring combinations.
- **Complete Scoring System:** Includes all standard Yahtzee scoring categories.
- **Interactive Scorecard:** Track scores and completed categories throughout the game.
- **Competitive Experience:** Challenge the AI and aim for the highest final score.

## 🎲 How to Play

1. Roll all five dice at the beginning of your turn.
2. Choose which dice you want to keep.
3. Roll the remaining dice again.
4. You can roll up to three times per turn.
5. Select an available scoring category based on your dice.
6. Take turns with the AI opponent until all scoring categories are completed.
7. The player with the highest total score wins!

## 🏆 Scoring Categories

### Upper Section

- Ones
- Twos

- Fours
- Fives
- Sixes

### Lower Section

- Three of a Kind
- Four of a Kind
- Full House
- Small Straight
- Large Straight
